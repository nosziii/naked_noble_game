## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges organic artisanal honesty with whimsical, feel-good indulgence. It speaks to health-conscious snack lovers, vegan dessert seekers, and contemporary foodies who appreciate clean ingredients without sacrificing joyful, treat-yourself delight.

The visual style blends **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Retro-Modern Playfulness**:
- Surfaces reflect the warmth of unbleached parchment and whipped botanical creams rather than sterile digital whites.
- Soft dusty terracotta and berry hues provide an inviting, sweet foundation derived from natural strawberry, hazelnut, and fruit purees.
- Contrast is established through rich forest pine green to anchor calls to action with botanical authority, counterbalanced by sunlit golden accents for celebratory moments and promotional game wins.
- Interface components feature soft, plump, touchable curvature evoking smooth jars, natural nut butters, and vintage mechanical slot reels.

## Colors

The palette directly references gourmet fruit spreads, botanical foliage, and game-show joy:

- **Primary (`#C27D88`)**: Soft dusty rose / terracotta used for hero containers, header ribbons, decorative badges, and warm focal surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#17433C`)**: Deep pine green provides authoritative contrast for primary CTAs, slot pull levers, key frame borders, and solid interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#F4B436`)**: Warm honey gold reserved for celebratory reward triggers, slot jackpot highlights, winning reel frames, starburst indicators, and score tallies.
- **Neutral (`#F9F5EE`)**: A soft, comforting warm cream canvas that removes screen glare while giving rich pantry food packaging tactile grounding.
- **Neutral Dark (`#241E1C`)**: Deep cocoa brown used for high-legibility body copy and micro-details, softer than harsh true black.
- **Surface Elevation Light (`#FFFDF9`)**: Pure whipped cream surface for inner reel windows, input fields, and product spotlight pods.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Epilogue** for display titles and gamified callouts with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for clean, readable body copy and instructional micro-text.

- **Epilogue**: Gives headers a distinctive, chubby retro-editorial weight. The heavy 700 and 800 weights create punchy, high-impact slot game signage and promotional slogans reminiscent of organic packaging labels.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Maintains soft, approachable proportions with open counters, guaranteeing instant legibility on game instructions, coupon claim codes, ingredient transparency lists, and checkout microcopy.
- Headlines should leverage tight leading and slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to produce cozy, unified blocks of punchy text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a hybrid centered gaming stage nested inside an adaptive responsive frame:

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: Max container width of 1200px. The promotional slot machine sits centered in an 8-column focal stage flanked by product teasers and reward prize lists in 2-column wings. Outer margin is `2rem` (`32px`) with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout. The slot machine occupies full width with interactive levers and spin buttons anchored directly below the reels. Gutters remain `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Mobile (under 768px)**: 4-column layout. Outer margin reduces to `1rem` (`16px`) with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters. Game controls shift to a thumb-friendly bottom-anchored sticky dock.
- Internal component rhythm follows multiples of 4px, where default element stacking uses `space-md` (`16px`) and card cluster padding uses `space-lg` (`24px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a tactile, layered approach inspired by wooden pantry shelves and vintage mechanical arcade cabinets:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Soft warm background `#F9F5EE` with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Product Cards & Content Pods)**: Tinted white `#FFFDF9` with a subtle, warm-tinted shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(184, 112, 124, 0.12)` accompanied by a crisp 1px border of `rgba(184, 112, 124, 0.2)`.
- **Level 2 (Slot Machine Housing & Floating Drawers)**: Deep dimensional lift with a layered drop shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(23, 67, 60, 0.18), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(23, 67, 60, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Win Banners & Game Modals)**: Golden celebratory halo: `0 16px 40px -4px rgba(244, 180, 54, 0.45), 0 0 0 3px #F4B436`.
- **Inner Depth (Reel Windows & Text Inputs)**: Recessed tactile inset effect created with `inset 0 2px 5px rgba(23, 67, 60, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped, generously rounded geometric signature (`roundedness: 3`):

- **Buttons & Chips**: Full pill curvature (`9999px` border radius) creating inviting, organic pebble-like touch points that feel friendly and safe.
- **Reel Windows & Game Cards**: Substantial corner smoothing using `rounded-xl` (`3rem` / `48px` on desktop containers, `1.5rem` / `24px` on mobile cards) echoing round jar lids and confectionery packaging.
- **Slot Machine Cabinet**: Pill-topped silhouette with organic archways and smoothly radiused frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Spin / CTA)**: Solid Pine Green (`#17433C`) background, cream (`#FFFDF9`) text, pill radius (`9999px`), 16px vertical by 32px horizontal padding. Bold Epilogue or Jakarta label. On hover, translates up 2px with an intensified pine glow. On active click, depresses 2px with an inner shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Terracotta outline (`2px solid #C27D88`), transparent fill, text `#C27D88`. Hover transitions to solid `#C27D88` with `#FFFDF9` text.
- **Jackpot / Golden Spin Button**: Vibrant gold (`#F4B436`) with deep pine text (`#17433C`). Includes a subtle shimmering pulse animation for maximum delight.

### Slot Machine Unit (Karos Játékgép)
- **Outer Shell**: Dusty rose (`#C27D88`) curved chassis with a deep pine green border (`3px solid #17433C`).
- **Reel Chamber**: Recessed cream (`#FFFDF9`) display containing 3 spinning windows showing spread jars, fruit icons (strawberries, coconut, hazelnuts), and golden stars. Separated by subtle vertical terracotta divider lines.
- **Physical Lever / Arm**: Vintage chrome or gold rod topped with a tactile round ball knob colored in pine green or terracotta that rotates downward on trigger.

### Win Banners & Celebration Toasts
- **Banner Housing**: Golden sunburst background (`#F4B436`) with rounded scalloped or pill edges, deep green typography (`#17433C`), flanked by retro star sparkle accents.
- **Coupon / Prize Card**: Cream parchment texture with dashed berry-colored border (`2px dashed #C27D88`), large voucher code, and one-tap copy button.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Recessed pill containers with off-white fill (`#FFFDF9`), border `1.5px solid rgba(194, 125, 136, 0.35)`.
- Focus state activates a 2px pine green glow ring (`#17433C`). Text is set in cocoa dark (`#241E1C`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped labels with soft pastel washes (e.g., 15% opacity terracotta or green background), solid matching colored text, 6px vertical by 14px horizontal padding, font-weight 700.
